Do not mention research methods.
Use only the input packet.
Do not invent facts or motives.

Goal:
Create a brief that helps a writer produce a page that answers the query immediately, feels state-specific, and avoids thin/template content.

Rules:

QUERY

- Must reflect the real search intent, not a broad topic label.

ANGLE

- Must identify the strongest editorial hook of the page.

FACTS

LINKS

- Format: /path — anchor text

AVOID
- Max 3 bullets.
- Focus on the biggest content risks.

- Answer first.
- Stay tightly on this symbol in this state.
- Favor concrete facts over generic description.
- Every section must earn its place.
- If a paragraph could fit another state with minor edits, it probably does not belong.
